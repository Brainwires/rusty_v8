V8 is unchanged from `v156.0.0-denext.2` (15.6.75.33); the crate is `156.0.0+denext.3`. This release hardens the binding's guard on V8's shared read-only heap (`snapshot::read_only_heap_guard`); it changes no public API.

- **A snapshot creator after the last isolate.** V8 frees the isolate group's read-only heap when the group's last isolate is disposed, and the next isolate sets up a new one. A snapshot creator that became that next isolate kept the new heap writable without the guard noticing, and regular isolates created on other threads attached to it (aborts and SIGSEGVs in `ReadOnlySpace` and `Deserializer::ReadReadOnlyHeapRef`). The guard now counts the group's isolates, and isolate creation waits while such a creator's window is open.
- **Isolates of snapshots with different read-only images.** V8 attaches every isolate to the group's heap whatever its own snapshot holds. An isolate from a snapshot whose creator extended the read-only heap, created while isolates of another image were alive, aborted deterministically (`vector[] index out of bounds` in `Deserializer::ReadReadOnlyHeapRef`). The guard now tells images apart by the read-only checksum in the blob header: an isolate of another image waits until the group's isolates are disposed and then sets the heap up from its own snapshot, and it panics with the cause where that wait could never end (its own thread owns an isolate of the other image) or outlasts 120 s.
- A process whose isolates all come from one snapshot never waits. Creating and disposing an isolate now takes the guard's mutex: about 46 ns per create and dispose uncontended, under the noise of creating an isolate (about 150 us).
- New tests: `test_snapshot_creator_after_last_isolate`, `test_snapshot_read_only_image`, and unit tests of the guard in `src/snapshot.rs`.

Details, the review and the remaining limits: DENEXT.md, section "Snapshot creators and concurrent isolates".
